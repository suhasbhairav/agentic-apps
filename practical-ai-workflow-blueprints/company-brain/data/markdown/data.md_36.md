### DATA DISCONNECT DIAGNOSTIC: CUSTOMER ONBOARDING PIPELINE

* **The Point of Failure:** Sales representatives capture extensive custom requirements during the discovery phase, but they record this context in unstructured text fields within local account documents.
* **Systemic Disconnect:** When a deal is marked "Closed-Won," the automated system transfers only five structured data fields (Company Name, Deal Value, License Count, Primary Contact, Industry) to the Onboarding team's tracking software.
* **The Impact:** 84% of the deep customer context gathered during the sales cycle is dropped during this automated system handoff. As a result, onboarding managers must spend the first two weeks of engagement re-interviewing the client, frustrating customers and lengthening onboarding timelines by 18 days.
* **Strategic Recommendation:** Modify the automated handoff process to compile sales discovery documents directly into the customer onboarding workspace, ensuring context is preserved across teams.
