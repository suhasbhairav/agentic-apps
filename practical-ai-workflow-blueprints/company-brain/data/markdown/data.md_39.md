### 7.1 Specialization of the Multi-Agent Network

Rather than relying on a single general-purpose AI model, Cerenovus deploys a network of specialized, coordinate agents, each focused on a specific aspect of organizational health:

1. **The Archival Historian Agent:** Focuses on tracking historical context across long time horizons. This agent cross-references past projects, archived email threads, and historical initiatives to prevent the company from repeating past mistakes.
2. **The Compliance Guardian Agent:** Continuously monitors the knowledge graph for data governance, privacy, and policy alignment. It flags when sensitive data drifts into unsecure communication spaces or when processes diverge from regulatory requirements.
3. **The Operations Optimizer Agent:** Constantly evaluates workflow efficiency. This agent analyzes system patterns to detect emerging bottlenecks, communication gaps, and process friction, surfacing proactive recommendations before operational issues impact the bottom line.
